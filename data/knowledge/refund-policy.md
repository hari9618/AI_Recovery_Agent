# Returns & Refund Policy

## Hardware returns

Unused hardware in its original packaging may be returned within 15 days of delivery. A 10%
restocking fee applies. Hardware that has been installed or commissioned is not returnable but is
covered by the 12-month manufacturer warranty.

## Pulse subscription refunds

Pulse platform subscriptions may be cancelled within 30 days of activation for a pro-rata refund of
the unused period. After 30 days, subscriptions are non-refundable for the current billing year.

## Services

Installation and training fees are refundable only if Arcline has not yet started the work.
Annual maintenance contracts are non-refundable once the contract period has started.

## Refund approvals

- Refunds up to ₹50,000 may be approved by the Sales Manager.
- Refunds above ₹50,000 require approval from Finance and the Owner.
- Sales representatives cannot approve or promise any refund.

All refunds are paid to the original payment method within 15 business days of approval.
