# Discount Authority Policy

This policy defines who may approve price reductions on quotations issued by Arcline Systems.
It applies to hardware, Pulse platform subscriptions and services. All percentages are applied to
the quotation subtotal before GST.

## Discount approval limits

| Total discount on the quotation | Who can approve |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | The account's sales representative, without further approval |
| Above 10% and up to 20% | Sales Manager approval is required before the revised quote is shared |
| Above 20% | Owner (Managing Director) approval is required |

Discounts above 10% require manager approval. A salesperson must not mention, promise or hint at
a discount above 10% to a customer in writing or verbally until the approval is recorded in the CRM.

## Conditions for granting a discount

A discount should be exchanged for something of value to Arcline. Acceptable reasons are:

- a multi-year Pulse subscription (two years or more),
- volume — five or more sites in a single order,
- 100% advance payment,
- a public case study or reference-site commitment,
- a documented competitive situation where the customer has shared a written competing quotation.

Discounts must not be offered simply because a customer asks. Before reducing price, the sales
representative should first re-establish value (see the Objection Handling Guide).

## Competitive situations

When a customer shares a lower competing quotation, the representative should request the written
competing quote and compare scope line by line. Competitor quotes commonly exclude commissioning,
operator training or platform alerts. If, after scope alignment, a discount above 10% is still needed
to win the deal, the representative raises a discount approval request with the competing quote attached.

## Items that cannot be discounted

- The Annual Maintenance Contract (SVC-AMC) is never discounted below its list price.
- Installation & commissioning (SVC-INSTALL) may be discounted by at most 10%, regardless of approver.

## Record keeping

Every approved discount must be recorded on the quotation with the approver's name and the reason
code. Revised quotations keep the same quote number with an incremented version.
