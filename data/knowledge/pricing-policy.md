# Pricing & Quotation Policy

| SKU | Item | List price |
|---|---|---|

| ARC-TH-05 | Temperature & Humidity Sensor | ₹4,600 per unit |
| PULSE-STD | Pulse Platform Standard | ₹1,20,000 per site per year |
| PULSE-ENT | Pulse Platform Enterprise | ₹2,40,000 per site per year |
| SVC-INSTALL | Installation & commissioning | ₹45,000 per site |

| SVC-TRAIN | Operator training | ₹18,000 per batch of 15 |

GST at 18% is charged in addition to all prices shown on a quotation.

## Quotation validity

Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue unless a shorter validity is stated on the
quote. An expired quotation must be re-issued as a new version before the customer can place an order;
prices on a re-issued quotation follow the price list in force on the re-issue date.

## Payment terms

Standard terms are 50% advance with the purchase order and 50% on commissioning. Pulse subscriptions
are billed annually in advance. Customers with an existing account in good standing may be offered
30-day credit on the balance with Finance approval.

## Price protection

If Arcline revises its price list while a valid quotation is outstanding, the quoted price is honoured
until the quotation's validity date.

## Platform licensing

Pulse licences are per site, not per user. Each licence includes unlimited dashboard users for that
site, alerting, monthly reports and API access (Enterprise only). Customers may start with one site
and add sites later at the then-current list price.
