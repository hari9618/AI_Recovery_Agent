# Customer Communication Guidelines

## Tone

Professional, warm and concise. Address the customer by first name if they have used ours. Avoid
jargon unless the customer used it first.

## Structure of a follow-up email

1. Reference the customer's last message or question specifically.
2. Answer every open question, or say exactly when the answer will come.
3. Propose one clear next step with a date (a call, a revised quote, a site visit).
4. Keep it under 180 words.

## What must never be written

- Commitments on price, discount, refund or delivery date that are outside policy or not yet approved.
- Any mention of a discount above 10% before manager approval is recorded.
- Negative statements about competitors.
- Internal information: margins, other customers' prices, internal approval discussions.
- Customer data belonging to another customer.

## Response time

Customer questions must be answered within 24 business hours (see the Sales Playbook).

## Signature

Every email ends with the representative's name, title, "Arcline Systems" and a direct phone number.
