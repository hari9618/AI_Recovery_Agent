# Quotation & Approval SOP

## Creating a quotation

1. Complete the site survey and record the number of sites, panels and assets.
2. Build the quote from the current price list; one quote number per opportunity.
3. Default validity is 30 days. Use a shorter validity (minimum 15 days) only for hardware-heavy quotes
   during supply constraints.
4. Send the quote from the CRM so that the sent date is recorded.

## Revising a quotation

Revisions keep the same quote number with an incremented version. Any revision that changes the total
discount above 10% must be approved by the Sales Manager before it is sent.

## High-value deals

Quotations of ₹3,00,000 or more are high-value. For high-value deals, any customer-facing commercial
communication (revised pricing, discounts, payment terms) must be reviewed by the Sales Manager before
it is sent.

## Expiring quotations

When a quotation is within 5 days of expiry and the customer has not decided, the representative should
contact the customer to confirm interest and, if required, re-issue the quote before it lapses.

## Approval records

Approvals are recorded in the CRM approval centre with the approver, timestamp and reason. Verbal
approvals are not valid.
