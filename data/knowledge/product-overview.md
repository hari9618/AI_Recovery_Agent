# Arcline Product Overview

## Edge Gateway GW-200

Industrial gateway that collects data from Arcline meters and sensors over Modbus and wireless, and
sends it securely to the Pulse platform. Supports Ethernet, Wi-Fi and 4G. One gateway typically covers
one plant building. The gateway can run on an existing Wi-Fi network, though a separate VLAN is
recommended for security.

## Sensors and meters

- **EM-3P** three-phase energy meter for panel-level energy monitoring.
- **VS-10** wireless vibration sensor for motors, pumps and compressors; detects bearing wear and
  imbalance weeks before failure.
- **TH-05** temperature & humidity sensor for cold rooms, warehouses and server rooms.

## Pulse Platform

Cloud dashboards, threshold and anomaly alerts (SMS, email, WhatsApp), monthly energy reports and
maintenance work-order integration. Enterprise adds multi-site benchmarking, API access and SSO.

## Installation and commissioning

Includes cabling from the gateway to panels within 30 metres, configuration of meters and sensors,
dashboard set-up and a handover session. Cabling beyond 30 metres is quoted separately.

## Typical outcomes

Customers commonly report an 8–12% reduction in energy cost in the first year and earlier detection
of mechanical faults. Outcomes vary by site and are not guaranteed.
